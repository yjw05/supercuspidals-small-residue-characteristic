# Research drafts

These drafts are shared for discussion and independent mathematical review. Their claims and proofs have not been independently verified for this repository. Neither draft claims a general exhaustion theorem for compact induction. Corrections and focused contributions are welcome through issues and pull requests.

## Congruence data and compact induction in small characteristic

Research note dated 2 November 2026 (78 pages). It studies congruence data, intertwining, compact-induction constructions, and obstructions to extracting inducing data from arbitrary supercuspidals. Its proposed constructions and proofs require further review; the general extraction and exhaustion problem remains open in the draft.

- [PDF](congruence-data.pdf)
- [Main TeX file](congruence-data-main.tex)
- [Complete TeX source (ZIP)](congruence-data-complete-source.zip)

The main file uses other TeX files. To compile it, unzip the complete source and run latexmk on weighted-root-heisenberg-refinement-note.tex.

## Finite algebra data for supercuspidal representations

Research draft dated 1 November 2026 (11 pages). It proposes finite algebra data at fixed level and central character. Its general construction gives a quotient of a compact induction; it does not claim that all representations are single compact inductions. See the draft for its exact hypotheses and effective-input conditions. Its arguments require further mathematical review.

- [PDF](finite-algebra-data.pdf)
- [TeX source](finite-algebra-data.tex)

The two PDFs and source files are shared as research drafts, not as a resolution of the general problem.
